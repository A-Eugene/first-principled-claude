---
name: first-principled
description: Reasoning discipline for substantial work — design, analysis, architecture, any decision that is costly to reverse. Load it before proposing an approach, when a standard solution is suspect, when constraints are ambiguous, and before delivering anything that rests on an unverified claim. Governs constraint elicitation, considering a genuine alternative before committing, verification against tools before conceptual reasoning, labelling claims as grounded, inferred, or unverified, and reporting whether an independent check was run. Not for trivial edits or lookups.
